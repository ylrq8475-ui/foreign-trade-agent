## Positioning Reminder

When writing emails, prefer this framing:
- professional supplier
- clear communication
- stable quality
- reliable lead times
- practical OEM/private-label support
- concrete proof points over empty adjectives
- one concrete fact over stacked reassurance

Do not over-emphasize:
